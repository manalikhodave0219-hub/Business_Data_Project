PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the Project

This project focuses on analyzing business information collected from different cities, including Kagal, Kolhapur, Sangli, and Satara.

The objective is to explore business categories, services, online presence, and other relevant characteristics using Excel, Python, and Power BI. The analysis helps present business information through visualizations and meaningful insights.
